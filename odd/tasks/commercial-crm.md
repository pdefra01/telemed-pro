# Commercial structure + CRM (supervisors/advisors, prospects, goals, collections)
Objective: a one-level commercial hierarchy (supervisor → advisors) with a lightweight CRM. Branch: feat/commercial-crm-prospects (delivery 1). Delivery: ask-on-risk, 4 deliveries. Runners: pgTAP (`supabase test db --local`), Vitest (`npm test`), node tests in server/__tests__.

## Deliveries
- D1: roles + prospects (this branch). D2: monthly goals + metrics. D3: collections follow-up. D4: automatic prospect intake (lead surveys, incomplete adhesions).

## Specs
- S1 (user): "implementar una estructura comercial y un crm (inicial, no complejo), que permita tener un esquema de supervisores/vendedores donde seguir sus metricas (actuales y otras a definir, poder tener una base de datos de prospectos"
- S2 (user): "el vendedor es el asesor actual" — reuse role `advisor`; no parallel seller model.
- S3 (user): "Un nivel alcanza" — add role `supervisor`; each advisor has at most one supervisor (`profiles.supervisor_id`). No deeper hierarchy.
- S4 (user): "Ambas, empezando por la manual" — D1 is manual prospect entry by the advisor; automatic intake is D4.
- S5 (user): "accesibilidad al CRM por parte del asesor tambien" — the advisor sees and edits only their own prospects; the supervisor sees their team's; the admin sees all (RLS).
- S6 (assumption): prospect pipeline `new → contacted → interested → affiliated | lost`, plus `last_contact_at` for "no follow-up" metrics (D2).
- S7 (assumption): the admin creates supervisors (server, like `/api/create-advisor`) and assigns advisors to a supervisor; `supervisor_id` is a privileged column the user cannot self-edit.
- S8 (user, later deliveries): monthly goals per advisor in affiliates, revenue or both, set by the supervisor for their team and by the admin for all (D2); collections status of the affiliates each advisor brought (`profiles.producer_id`, view `affiliate_payment_status`), with collection-contact logging, always visible to the supervisor (D3): "el CRM debe permitir seguimiento de las cobranzas de los afiliados que trajo cada uno, con escala a su supervisor" / "el supervisor conoce la situacion sea la que sea" / "Sí, que registre gestiones de cobro".

- S9 (user, D2): "Cuota mensual del plan" — revenue generated by a sale = the plan's `monthly_cost` (not first-period amount).
- S10 (assumption, D2): monthly goal = one row per advisor and month with an optional affiliates target and an optional revenue target (at least one); set by the supervisor for their team and by the admin for all; the advisor reads only their own. Stacked on delivery 1: branch feat/commercial-crm-goals is based on feat/commercial-crm-prospects (PR #95).
- S11 (assumption, D2): metrics per advisor and month: affiliates sold (approved adhesion requests with `promoter_id` = advisor `promoter_code`, month = COALESCE(activated_at, created_at)); revenue (sum of `monthly_cost` of each sale's plan via approved_profile_id → profiles.plan_id; sales with no resolvable plan count as affiliates but add 0 revenue); prospects loaded in the month (cohort) with how many are contacted-or-beyond and affiliated, and the conversion rate affiliated/loaded; open prospects without follow-up for 7 days or more (snapshot, not by month); links shared (cumulative `producers.links_shared_count`); progress against the goals. Collections/mora belong to D3.

- S12 (user, D3): "el CRM debe permitir seguimiento de las cobranzas de los afiliados que trajo cada uno, con escala a su supervisor"; clarified: "con escala me referia a que el supervisor conoce la situacion sea la que sea" (no thresholds, no escalation action: the supervisor always sees every status of their team's affiliates); "Sí, que registre gestiones de cobro" (the advisor logs calls, notes and promises to pay).
- S13 (assumption, D3): the affiliates an advisor brought = profiles with `producer_id` = the advisor, plus the approved_profile_id of adhesion requests whose `promoter_id` = the advisor's `promoter_code` (same attribution as D2 sales). The collections view lists them with every status (current/pending/overdue), balance, coverage end, last contact and last promise. Collection actions are append-only (no edit/delete), types call/whatsapp/visit/note/promise (a promise needs a date). Advisor: reads and logs for their affiliates; supervisor: reads their team's; admin: all. Branch feat/commercial-crm-collections is stacked on feat/commercial-crm-goals (PR #96).
